# Awesome-Reputation-Management

## Top Reputation Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Online Review Monitoring, Response, Generation, Listings Management, Sentiment Analysis & Local Reputation*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Reputation Management**. These systems help businesses monitor, respond to, and generate online reviews across Google, Facebook, Yelp, industry sites, and social channels while managing listings and analyzing customer sentiment.



**Examples** include Birdeye, Podium, Reputation.com, NiceJob, Broadly, ReviewTrackers, Grade.us, Mention, Brand24, and LocalClarity (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured reputation and review-management platforms are almost entirely commercial. Open options are limited to general social listening, web monitoring, review scraping experiments, and content-moderation tools. This section lists the strongest available resources and is realistic about the significant gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Social listening and mention monitoring open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects that scrape or aggregate public mentions from social platforms and the web for basic brand monitoring.



- **[Review aggregation and scraping experiments](https://github.com/)**  

  Open scripts and libraries that collect public reviews from Google, Yelp, or other sources (use carefully and respect terms of service).



- **[Sentiment analysis open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  NLP toolkits (spaCy, Hugging Face models, VADER, etc.) used to score review and mention sentiment in custom pipelines.



- **[Content moderation and review-queue platforms (e.g. Coop)](https://github.com/roostorg/coop)**  

  Open-source moderation dashboards and rule engines that can be adapted for review response workflows.



- **[Self-hosted notification and alert systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools that watch RSS, APIs, or search results and alert teams to new reviews or mentions.



- **[Google Business Profile / listings open helpers](https://github.com/)**  

  Community scripts for managing or auditing local listings data where APIs permit.



- **[Customer feedback open forms and portals](https://github.com/)**  

  Open survey and feedback collection tools that feed into a broader reputation process.



- **[Dashboard and BI open stacks for review data](https://github.com/)**  

  Metabase, Superset, or custom dashboards that visualize aggregated review and sentiment data.



- **[CRM + review workflow open integrations](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight connectors that push new reviews into open CRMs or ticketing systems for response tracking.



- **[Academic and research reputation / trust models](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental systems exploring trust scores, contributor reputation, or decentralized review concepts.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Building a lightweight monitoring stack with open sentiment models + scheduled scrapers or official APIs (where available).

- Using open social listening tools or RSS/search alerts for basic brand mention tracking.

- Combining open feedback forms with manual review response processes for small businesses.

- Accepting that broad multi-site review coverage, automated generation campaigns, AI response suggestions, listings syndication, and enterprise reporting still require commercial platforms.

- Focusing open efforts on internal response workflows and analytics rather than full replacement of review platforms.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Collect public reviews/mentions via permitted APIs or careful scrapers → run open sentiment analysis → store in a database → surface in an open dashboard → route responses through a ticketing or CRM system. This provides basic visibility. Commercial platforms (Birdeye, Podium, Reputation.com, ReviewTrackers, NiceJob, Brand24, etc.) remain the practical choice for reliable multi-site monitoring, review generation at scale, listings management, and polished team workflows.
